Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Imageye is designed to respect your privacy. The free extension does not require an account, an email address, or a payment. The limited personal-information processing described below applies only when a user chooses to purchase, activate, restore, or manage Imageye PRO.

1. Free Extension: No Account or Personal Information Collection

Imageye's standard image-search and download features work without registration. When you use these features, Imageye processes information from the webpage you choose to scan, including image resources and URLs, within your browser to display and download images at your request.

Imageye does not collect or store your browsing history, the images you find, or the content of the pages you scan on Imageye's servers as part of this standard functionality. Imageye does not use these activities for advertising profiles or behavioral tracking.

Extension settings and preferences may be stored in your browser so that your choices are remembered. This is local storage, not a customer profile maintained by Imageye.

2. Optional Imageye PRO: Information Processed

Only users who choose to use the optional PRO purchase or account-recovery flow need to provide an email address. The email address is used to process the purchase and associate or restore access to PRO features. It is processed by our checkout/backend flow and by our payment provider, Stripe.

Imageye does not store plaintext customer email addresses in its Firebase/Firestore PRO entitlement database. For entitlement identification, our backend converts the email address into a cryptographic hash and stores the hash instead of the plaintext email address. A hash is a pseudonymous identifier and is not necessarily anonymous data.

Stripe processes payments and may store the email address, billing information, and transaction records under its own privacy policy. Imageye does not store payment card details in its Firestore entitlement database.

3. Why PRO Information Is Used

PRO-related information is used only to initiate and confirm purchases, manage subscription or lifetime entitlements, validate PRO access, restore access when requested, provide purchase-related support, process cancellations or refunds, and maintain the security of these services.

4. Data Handling, Storage, and Security

Imageye uses Google Firebase/Firestore to maintain PRO entitlement records, such as hashed email identifiers, purchase or subscription status, and the information needed to validate access. These records are separate from the webpage images processed locally by the extension.

Our checkout and backend communications use secure connections. Access to PRO entitlement records is restricted to the systems and authorized operators needed to provide and support the service. Service providers may process necessary technical request information to operate and protect their infrastructure.

5. Third-Party Services and Sharing

For optional PRO functionality, Imageye uses Stripe for payment processing and subscription management, and Google Firebase / Google Cloud for backend processing and entitlement storage. These providers process information necessary for their services under their applicable privacy terms.

We do not sell personal information, share browsing history for marketing, or use PRO purchase details for behavioral advertising. We may disclose information where required by law.

6. Retention, Your Choices, and Deletion

You can continue using Imageye's free functionality without purchasing PRO or providing an email address. You may remove locally stored extension information by clearing the relevant browser data or uninstalling the extension, subject to your browser's storage behavior.

PRO entitlement records are retained for as long as necessary to provide or restore purchased access and handle support, refunds, security, and applicable legal obligations. Stripe retains payment and transaction information according to its own policies and legal obligations.

To ask about personal information associated with PRO, or to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable, contact us using the email address below. Some records may need to be retained for legal, tax, accounting, or fraud-prevention reasons.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to Imageye, its PRO services, or applicable requirements. The date above indicates the latest update. We will communicate material changes when required.
